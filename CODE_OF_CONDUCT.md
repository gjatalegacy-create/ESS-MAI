# Code of conduct

ESS-MAI public research discussions are expected to be respectful, precise, and evidence-first.

## Expected conduct

- Address claims, source, experiments, and methods rather than people.
- Welcome successful reproductions, failed reproductions, counterexamples, and corrections when they are documented clearly.
- Distinguish observed behavior from interpretation, attribution, and future hypotheses.
- Respect the project's publication, privacy, security, and authority boundaries.
- Give accurate credit to prior work and public contributors.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate misrepresentation, credential exposure, publication of private material, spam, and repeated unverifiable promotion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Security-sensitive or private conduct reports may be sent to [gjata@legacy.al](mailto:gjata@legacy.al).

This code governs participation in this repository; it is not a statement that every research disagreement has one required interpretation.
