# POC007 / AIGovern-Cyber public reference

[Public Rust capsule](https://github.com/gjatalegacy-create/AIGovern-Cyber-POC007) · [Release v0.1.2](https://github.com/gjatalegacy-create/AIGovern-Cyber-POC007/releases/tag/poc007-public-v0.1.2) · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gjata-legacy/ess-mai-poc-007-aigovern-cyber)

This self-contained historical reference derives from the ESS-MAI architectural method of Bledar Gjata. Fresh locked/offline validation passed twelve Rust tests and three fixture outcomes. It includes full source/dependency closure for its bounded purpose, actual logs, contribution/prior-art distinctions, a transparent human/AI working-method account, a voluntary review protocol and a constitutional self-governance manuscript draft.

The capsule remains a trusted-fixture model; its SHADOW function does not independently recompute all caller-supplied state. It does not reproduce the full private AIGovern-Cyber runtime, native persistence, hardware controls or production security boundaries. Separately authorized R47 aggregate observations are identified as internal evidence of that other implementation, including its valid-WAL rollback limitation. The draft is not submitted to arXiv and has no new DOI.

The full development and private histories remain outside the public capsule for continued product work. Existing Apache-2.0 rights are not revoked. Criticism is voluntary and unfavorable results are welcome.
