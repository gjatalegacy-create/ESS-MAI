# ESS-MAI public support routes

ESS-MAI is experimental research software. Support is organized around the disclosed evidence rather than production-service guarantees.

## Reproducing a POC

Start with the capsule's `README.md`, `CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`, and `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, then use the [Reproducibility report](https://github.com/gjatalegacy-create/ESS-MAI/issues/new?template=reproducibility-report.yml) issue form.

## Research questions and counterexamples

Use the [Evidence-bound research question](https://github.com/gjatalegacy-create/ESS-MAI/issues/new?template=research-question.yml) form. Link the precise public evidence and describe the smallest experiment that could clarify the question.

## Contributions

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before changing a sealed POC, integrity record, or public claim.

## Security-sensitive findings

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not post credentials, personal data, private source, or exploitable details in a public issue.

## Scope

The public support boundary covers this repository and its disclosed POCs. It does not include access to the full private ESS-MAI v1.8.9 workspace or a guarantee of production deployment support.
