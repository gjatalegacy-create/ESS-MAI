# Claim Boundary

## Supported by this public capsule's execution

- The disclosed four-crate workspace compiles with Cargo in locked offline mode.
- Twenty whole-file extracts match their v1.8.9 origins by SHA-256 and size.
- The 124-line Shadow selection excerpt matches the identified v1.8.9 line range after normalized-LF comparison.
- All 84 disclosed unit and integration tests pass.
- A GCL directive for the wrong phase is rejected by the extracted Light and Quantum state machines.
- The extracted Light Besa client launches a separate Shadow selector process and accepts a complete empty selection.
- The extracted Quantum Asht client launches a second Shadow selector process.
- With an empty selector state, Asht fails closed because no exact positive candidate exists.
- The empty-state route does not reach the post-Asht probe and does not execute a production Shadow commit.
- In the harness-only exact-pair positive control, the same Asht path succeeds and the post-Asht probe is reached.
- Three fresh empty-state runs reproduce the same result.

## Supported only as private historical context

The earlier detached native v1.8.9 capsule built the full private closure and reproduced a related cold-start blocker. Its full source and detailed structural manifest are deliberately excluded here. Its package-test totals are not test results of this public capsule.

## Supported by static source mapping, not public execution

- The private v1.8.9 source contains downstream Shadow judgment, positive/negative write construction, transaction, WAL, and terminal-evidence paths.
- The ordinary cold-start route fails before those downstream paths.
- Existing downstream writer presence does not by itself establish generation-zero reachability.

These statements identify the advancement seam. The full downstream runtime is not disclosed or executed here.

## Not supported

- This capsule does not execute the full production Light, Quantum, or Shadow binaries.
- The surgical Shadow selector is not the production Shadow core.
- The positive control is not a production fix, seed, verdict, or commit.
- No production Shadow judgment, `VaultTransaction`, WAL commit, terminal receipt, or external user output is executed here.
- The POC does not establish complete system end-to-end success.
- It does not establish that the proposed genesis route is sufficient; later failures may remain.
- It does not establish novelty, patentability, freedom to operate, security certification, or external validation.
- It does not grant any sibling authority the sovereignty of GCL.

## Terminology

`PASS` means the stated bounded command or assertion succeeded. `FAIL-CLOSED` means the system refused to continue at the observed gate. Neither label expands the claim beyond the disclosed procedure.
