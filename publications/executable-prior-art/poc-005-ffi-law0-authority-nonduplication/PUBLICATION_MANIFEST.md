# Publication manifest

## Included

- theory, scientific report, prior-art comparison and claim boundary;
- byte-identical focal source files;
- clearly labelled detached compilation adapters;
- Cargo manifests and lockfile;
- executable falsification experiment;
- Apache-2.0 license and citation metadata;
- CodeMeta machine-readable metadata;
- compact native/detached build, test, runtime and identity evidence;
- source-identity verification script.

## Excluded

- the complete ESS-MAI v189 source tree;
- Cargo `target/` directories and dependency caches;
- native or detached executables;
- unrelated C01/C02 audit capsules;
- private reference documents;
- credentials, tokens, machine-specific absolute source paths;
- any claim that a DOI or archive exists before it is actually issued.

## Artifact classification

```text
THEORY_POC
PARTIAL_MATERIALIZATION
EXECUTABLE_PRIOR_ART_CANDIDATE
```

The focal source is evidence. The experiment is new POC glue. Prior-art
references establish the claim boundary. A green build does not convert the
documented `CompatibilityHold` into a successful write.
