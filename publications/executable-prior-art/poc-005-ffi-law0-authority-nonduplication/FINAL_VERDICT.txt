PROVEN:
1. For one issued generation, copying the C-visible handle does not duplicate the Rust-owned one-shot authority.
2. Forged, unknown, empty, serial-replay and concurrent-copy cases reach the pre-registered refusal/hold states.
3. The direct FFI route does not silently write knowledge; it terminates at CompatibilityHold without the superior committed verdict.

NOT_PROVEN:
1. Durable authority consumption across process restart.
2. Semantic idempotency across fresh generations or an idempotent knowledge sink.
3. Default-production deployment or end-to-end FFI-to-Vault persistence.

COUNTEREXAMPLES:
1. Two fresh generations issued for the same module and payload each cross local authorization, so generation-local non-duplication is not semantic non-duplication.
2. A locally authorized non-empty call returns CompatibilityHold instead of a committed knowledge-write success.

ADVANCEMENT_METHOD:
Bind a durable semantic authorization key to the existing confirmed-verdict transaction, consume it atomically at the authoritative Shadow sink, make that sink idempotent, and verify restart/replay in a successor POC. Preserve the hierarchy: the FFI child carries evidence but never assumes verdict or persistence authority.
